Brittany Mederos

Hi, I'm Brittany! I'm into design, AI, building strong teams, good food, being a mom, and the ocean.

Currently, I'm a Principal Design & Research Manager at Microsoft, leading a 0-to-1 product team building transformative learning experiences powered by AI. I also co-run Enchant, a family health product studio making it easier for families to thrive.

Previously, I helped ship Microsoft Designer and its integration into Copilot, pioneered AI features in PowerPoint, and led design for Outlook and StaffHub. Before Microsoft, I designed supply chain tools at Elementum and crafted experiences for government at the CFPB.
